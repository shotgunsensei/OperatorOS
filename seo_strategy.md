# SEO Strategy — OperatorOS

## Target site
operatoros.net — Next.js 13+ App Router monorepo (apps/web + apps/api)

## In scope
- Public marketing pages: `/`, `/pricing`, `/how-it-works`, `/ecosystem`, `/modules`, `/privacy`, `/terms`
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, structured data, social meta

## Out of scope
- Authenticated console pages: `/app/**`, `/admin/**`
- SSO / invite flows: `/sso`, `/invites/**`
- Share-by-link pages: `/portfolio`, `/john` (intentionally noindexed)
- Module sub-apps hosted on separate subdomains (e.g., techdeck.operatoros.net)

## Target audience
SaaS operators and business teams evaluating a modular command-layer platform.

## Primary keywords
- modular business operating system
- SaaS command layer
- business operations platform
- OperatorOS
